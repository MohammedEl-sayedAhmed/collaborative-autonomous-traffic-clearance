# NNNN. Short title of the decision

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** name(s)

## Context and problem statement

What is the situation, the forces at play, and the problem we need to decide on?
Keep it factual and short.

## Considered options

- Option A
- Option B
- Option C

## Decision

The option chosen, stated plainly ("We will …"), and the key reasons why.

## Consequences

- **Positive:** what gets better / becomes possible.
- **Negative / trade-offs:** what gets harder, what we accept, follow-up work.
- **Neutral:** notable side effects.
